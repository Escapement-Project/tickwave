How it works

For a Saleae export, the converter first checks that every edge sits on the sample grid, in order. Then it goes through the capture three times: once to look for exact integer codes in each analog channel, once to encode, and once to check the recording against the capture, edge by edge and sample by sample. A recording that fails the check is never offered to save.

It reads a Saleae export a slice at a time, so the export can be larger than the memory the converter has. A sigrok or VCD file is read whole, and the recording is built in memory, so both have to fit. The converter runs on one thread, so a large capture takes longer here than with the command-line converter.
