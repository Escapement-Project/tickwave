Open a recording

The viewer shows a .tickwave recording from its address, or from files on this computer. A capture from Saleae Logic 2, sigrok or a VCD file converts here first.

The converter runs in your browser, on the same code as the command-line converter: the same capture and settings give the same file. The viewer's doc says how to move, zoom and measure in a recording.

Open another recording

Or open files from this computer: a .tickwave recording, with its sidecar's .toml and .jsonl files if it has them. Or convert a capture: a Saleae export's folder, or a sigrok .sr or .vcd file. A .zip of any of these opens too.

You can drop them here too. They stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

To try it, open a debug probe starting an SWD session, or an SWD bus on a mixed-signal analyser's analog inputs.

Each recording heads its own rows. Drag its grip, by its name, sideways to move the whole recording in time and line it up with the others: an edge of it lands on another's edge when they come within a few pixels, and anywhere with Alt held. Its Aligned by field takes the time too. A channel's own grip moves only that channel, and keeps it among its recording's rows. Times here are each recording's own, such as -40 ns. A recording's generated annotations come with it; annotations written by hand don't, as their times are the recording's own.

Drag a row's grip in the view sideways to move the channel in time, or up and down to change its place; drag the bars at its data's ends to trim it. Times here are the capture's, such as -40 ns or 1.2 ms.
