tickwave

Logic and mixed-signal captures, in a web page

tickwave shows a logic or mixed-signal capture inside a web page, beside the text that cites it.

It draws digital channels from their edges and analog channels from their samples, on one time axis. A phrase in the text can name a stretch of the capture: point at it to see that stretch, or click it to open the whole capture there. The Escapement Project's pages show their bench's recordings with it.

Reading a capture

Zoom, measure and place cursors, on one time axis

  • Move and zoom

    Ctrl or Cmd with the wheel, or a pinch, zooms about the pointer. A drag or a sideways swipe moves along the capture.

  • Read a pulse

    Point at a digital channel for the pulse's width, period, duty and frequency, or at an analog channel for its value.

  • Cursors and spans

    Two clicks place cursors and give the interval between them. A drag selects a span and measures a channel's clock in it.

  • Annotations

    A sidecar names spans and points, shown above the channels. Step through one kind of them, such as every WAIT.

Convert

From a Saleae Logic 2 export, a sigrok session or a VCD file

  1. Get the converter

    $cargo build --release -p tickwave-cli

    From tickwave's repository, with a current Rust toolchain. Or convert on the Open page, in your browser, on the same code.

  2. Convert

    $tickwave convert sigrok session.sr -o session.tickwave

    A Saleae export and a VCD file convert the same way. It checks what it wrote against the capture, edge by edge.

  3. Describe

    $tickwave info session.tickwave

    The recording's length and resolution, its channels and their edges.

Each time is a whole number of the capture's own ticks, so a recording never shows more resolution than its source had. The converter's doc has every option.

tickwave-view
A whole recording, closed to one line until the reader opens it.
tickwave-inset
One annotation's span, magnified, with a reading under it.
tickwave-ref
A phrase that cites an annotation. Pointing at it opens an inset.
tickwave-chart
Measurements against a setting. A point can open the recording behind it.

A view fetches only the part of a recording it shows, and nothing loads until the reader reaches it. The demo is a page built this way.
