tickwave shows logic and mixed-signal captures. It draws digital channels from their edges and analog channels from their samples, on one time axis.

The Escapement Project shows its bench's wire recordings with it: on Verge's measurements page, a chart's point opens the recording behind it. It opens your own recordings too, and nothing you open is uploaded. Try the demo to see it in a page.

It reads .tickwave recordings. The converter makes them from a Saleae Logic 2 binary export, a sigrok session or a VCD file. Give the viewer a recording's address, or open one from this computer. The viewer's doc says how to move, zoom and measure in it.
